## Rules for every letter

- One page. About 450 to 550 words in English, 500 to 600 in French.
- No em-dashes. Use a colon, a comma, or a new sentence.
- Every number must exist in `resume.json` or in `notes.md` under "Evidence you can cite".
- Do not include: salary, availability unless asked, the €30M and $2B claims, the HR matter, the disclosure-mandate project.
- Keep the gap paragraph. Reviewers remember honesty more than a fourth achievement.
- Write the source here first, render to PDF second. The `.md` is the version of record.
- Copy this file to `cover-letters/<yyyy-mm-dd>-<company>-<role>.md`, fill the front matter, and link the final PDF from the matching `applications/` folder.
